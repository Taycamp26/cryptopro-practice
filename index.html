The CryptoPRO support workflow

01 · Ask

What happened?

Bought, held, sold, exchanged, spent, earned, received as a gift, or transferred?

02 · Clarify

How did they acquire it?

Purchase, wages, independent services, mining, staking, gift, inheritance? What records or forms do they have?

03 · Connect

Which familiar tax category?

W-2 wages, Schedule C business income, other income, or investment disposition. There can be two events.

04 · Guide

Where does it go?

Explain the general rule and the appropriate software section. The customer supplies and determines their tax facts.

Stop and refer when needed: disputed or unknown basis; business versus hobby classification; collectibles; foreign asset disclosures; complex gifts/inheritance; losses from scams or bankruptcies; unusual adjustments. Use your approved escalation procedure.

Five questions that unlock most cases

  1. What did you actually do with the digital asset?
  2. How and when did you originally acquire it?
  3. Did you receive it as compensation, a reward, a gift, or an investment purchase?
  4. What records, statements, and tax forms do you have?
  5. Are you asking where to enter information, or asking us to decide a tax position?

Remember: the document received is evidence of a transaction, not always the answer to its tax classification.
